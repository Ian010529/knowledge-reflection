Phase 1 read only protocol.md, reference_input.json, and the two required root-policy entry files. Those policy files include historical aggregate quality/status summaries; no historical extraction, reference, individual judgment, trial files, candidate files, author scripts, or network sources were read. The reference was authored and SHA-256 frozen at 2026-09-24T11:48:52.883022+00:00 before any candidate access. Phase 2 read input_papers.json and initial.json after the freeze. Neither reference nor candidates were edited. Python performed serialization, quote/ID/hash validation only; all semantic decisions were model-authored.

50 candidates: 49 supported; 1 out_of_scope (P1179 r08, future research selection rather than a material/application finding). No unambiguous modality overclaim/underreport was found in retained candidates. Synthesis method-to-product statements in P1962 r01/r04 and procedural dispersion results in P1179 have claim-specific physical procedures, sufficient for their experimental labels; this does not justify labeling unrelated structural or performance results experimental. The frozen reference used unspecified for the two synthesis claims; that conservative modality omission is a reference annotation limitation, not a candidate error.

25 frozen reference claims: 22 complete, 2 partial, 1 missing. The missing calculated copper-ion hopping pseudo-potential is the main substantive omission. Partial coverage concerns module-A similarity and printed-device durability/adaptability. Candidate conditions were counted as represented content (e.g. P0450 thermal conductivity and P0261 Seebeck value); a fact merely appearing somewhere in a quotation was not automatically counted as an extracted relationship. Reference claims sometimes group jointly reported outcomes, so these are compact claim-coverage units, not 25 uniformly atomic edges. Keep this qualification in metrics and do not present as graph-edge recall.

Reference limitations: P0261 g09 combines Seebeck and deformation stability under the main intrinsic scope; the latter alone is peripheral. P0261 g01 fabrication suitability can reasonably be labeled method/model or mechanical scope, both peripheral. These scope/granularity ambiguities are disclosed without editing the frozen reference. Source redox/fraction corruption is retained and flagged by candidates; no new chemical identity is inferred, so legible source-relative claims remain supported.

Outside the four reference papers, P0232 has an explicit but unspecified-form relationship between interface heat generation and the temperature dependence of open-circuit potential, studied through multicomponent transport equations. A directionless/theoretical relationship could be retained with its model context; the candidate's empty list is overly conservative about requiring a direction or formula. This is a qualitative omission only, not an additional scored reference. P1179 also states a design purpose for PC/EMIM-TFSI mixing (improving both viscosity and conductivity), which is absent as a purpose-qualified relation. No additional obvious omissions were identified in P1064 or P0228 under the compact scope.
